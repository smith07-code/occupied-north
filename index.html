About this project

Eelam Tamil territories remain heavily militarised, with large areas still under heavy presence of Sri Lanka's armed forces.

By visualising these documented military installations and checkpoints in the Eelam Tamil territories, this site intends to create awareness among people worldwide.

What we track

🟢 Military presence — Documented army installations and checkpoints across the region.

🔵 Naval & air installations — Coastal and airbase footprints in the Northern Province.

🟠 Buddhist statues & temples — Newly built or disputed religious installations in historically Tamil areas.
