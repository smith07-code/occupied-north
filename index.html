The Purpose of this Website

Eelam Tamil territories remain heavily militarised with large areas still under heavy presence of Sri Lanka’s armed forces.

By visualising the presence of these armed men in a state which describes itself as a free and equal nation this website intends to create awareness to the people worldwide.
