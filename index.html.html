About

The purpose of this website

Eelam Tamil territories remain heavily militarised with large areas still under heavy presence of Sri Lanka’s armed forces.

By visualising these documented military installations and check points in the Eelam Tamil territories, This site intends to create awareness to people worldwide.

Documenting the ground truth of the Northern Province, one location at a time.
